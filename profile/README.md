## Hi there 👋

**Asclevor Technologies** is building developer-first infrastructure for medical knowledge.

Our first product focuses on **semantic retrieval of clinically similar published patient cases** — instead of keyword-searching through large volumes of literature, you describe a clinical presentation and get back the most similar published cases, with similarity scores and PubMed IDs (PMIDs).

### What we're working on

- 🧠 **167,000+ patient cases** sourced from PubMed data, processed and embedded with `BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5`
- 🔍 **Semantic similarity search** working locally, with a deployed database (~40,000 cases indexed)
- 🚧 **Public API** — in development
- 🎯 Built for **developers, researchers, and medical professionals** working at the intersection of medicine and software

### What we care about

- Modern, developer-friendly APIs
- Structured, easy-to-integrate responses
- Making medical knowledge as accessible to developers as modern infrastructure makes computing

> ⚠️ Asclevor's tools retrieve information. They do not diagnose, treat, or replace clinical judgment.
